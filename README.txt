Connect your PetSafe Smart Feed automatic feeder to Homey and make feeding part of your smart home. Check the food level and battery at a glance, give an extra portion from anywhere, and let Homey warn you before your pet's bowl stays empty.

Feed on demand
Give one portion with the Feed now button on the device tile, as a quick action, or from a flow. Combine it with other devices, for example to feed your pet when you are running late or when you leave the house.

Dashboard widget
Add the feeder widget to a Homey dashboard to see the food level and the last feeding at a glance. One tap on the widget gives your pet a portion.

Know when to refill
The feeder reports when food is running low and when it has run out. Use these in flows to send yourself a notification, add food to your shopping list, or flash a light in the kitchen. The time of the last feeding is shown on the device, so you can see whether your pet was fed today.

Keep an eye on power
See the battery level, get an alarm when the batteries are low, and know when the feeder is running without its power adapter.

Child lock
Lock or unlock the buttons on the feeder from Homey, or do it automatically from a flow.

Flow cards
Triggers: food is running low, food is no longer running low, food ran out, food was refilled, plus the standard battery, power and lock cards.
Conditions: food is or is not running low, food has or has not run out, the feeder is locked.
Actions: feed now, lock, unlock.

Easy setup
Sign in with the email address of your MyPetSafe account. PetSafe sends you a pincode by email; there is no password to enter. All feeders in your account can then be added in one go.

Requirements
A PetSafe Smart Feed feeder that is set up in the MyPetSafe app, and an internet connection. The app uses the PetSafe cloud and updates the feeder status every 5 minutes.
